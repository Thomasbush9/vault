Language originates from the need to share perceptual stimuli in an efficient way. Thus, resolving a bottleneck in communication (we are constrained in both tot sound we can emit and the time+processing). Words/sentences are good to compress the statistical dynamics of perceptual stimuli into discrete concepts that are mapped to those dynamics in both systems (the speaker and listener). 

Experiment one: 
Can we show that in a system with such constraints, a language-like system is developed? 
- Study human language constraints 
- Study ratio between perception and linguistic information 
- Study time difference 
- Study statistical compression
